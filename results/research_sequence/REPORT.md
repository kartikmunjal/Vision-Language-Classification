## 6. Robustness audit

At frame 7, occlusion reduced retrieval Recall@1 to 48.33%, with a 46.67% top-result flip rate. A negated caption scored at least as highly as the original on 74.33% of examples; subject-count swaps did so on 41.33% (n=300). This is a controlled audit, not a safety certification.

## Bottom line

The strongest positive result is that disagreement targeting improves downstream classification after actual retraining, especially for human presence and subject count. The strongest failure result is that naïve hard-negative mining catastrophically damages retrieval. Controlled corruption exposes substantial negation blindness and occlusion sensitivity. Natural-video validation remains the next required external-data step.
